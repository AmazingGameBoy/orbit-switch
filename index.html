1. Introduction

Welcome to Orbit Switch ("we," "us," "our," or "the App"). We respect your privacy and are committed to being transparent about how information is handled when you use Orbit Switch.

This Privacy Policy explains what information is collected, how it is used, where it may be stored, when certain information may be publicly displayed, and how you can delete your account and associated data.

Orbit Switch follows a data-minimization approach. Core gameplay is available in Guest Mode without requiring a cloud-connected account.

2. Information We Collect

Account Identifier

If you voluntarily sign in using Google Sign-In, authentication is handled through Google Identity services and Supabase Authentication. We use an authenticated user identifier to operate your Orbit Switch cloud account.

We do not request or store your Google account password.

Player Callsign / Username

If you create a cloud-connected profile, you may choose a player Callsign or Username. Your selected Callsign may be displayed publicly on Global Leaderboards.

Please do not use sensitive personal information, such as your real name, address, phone number, or other private information, as your public Callsign.

Gameplay and Progress Data

We may store gameplay-related information necessary to provide game functionality, cloud synchronization, and leaderboard features.

Progress

Player level, XP, legend stars, campaign progress and stage stars.

Scores

High scores, rankings and gameplay statistics.

Game Items

Virtual currency, unlocked cosmetics and equipped items.

Game Systems

Daily rewards, quests, streaks and power-up progression.

3. Information We Do Not Intentionally Collect

Based on the current functionality of Orbit Switch, we do not intentionally collect the following categories of information directly through the App:

  • Real name
  • Phone number
  • Physical address
  • Precise or approximate location
  • Contacts or address book
  • Photos or videos
  • Files stored on your device
  • Camera data
  • Microphone recordings
  • SMS messages or call logs
  • Payment card information
  • Advertising ID for advertising purposes
  • Device IMEI, serial number, or MAC address
  • Health information

4. Optional Google Sign-In

Orbit Switch allows users to optionally sign in using Google Sign-In. Signing in is optional for core gameplay.

Google Sign-In is used to:

  • Authenticate the player
  • Access a cloud-connected Orbit Switch account
  • Synchronize supported gameplay progress
  • Enable Global Leaderboard participation

Orbit Switch does not collect or store your Google account password.

5. How We Use Information

Information is used to:

  • Provide and operate Orbit Switch
  • Authenticate cloud-connected accounts
  • Synchronize supported gameplay progress
  • Maintain player profiles
  • Operate Global Leaderboards
  • Calculate player rankings
  • Maintain game progression
  • Protect account security and system integrity
  • Process account deletion requests
  • Respond to support inquiries
We do not sell, rent, or trade personal information. Orbit Switch does not currently use personal information for targeted advertising.

6. Global Leaderboards

If you choose to participate in Global Leaderboards, certain gameplay information may be visible to other players.

Public leaderboard information may include:

  • Player Callsign or Username
  • Global ranking
  • Current level
  • Legend stars
  • High score
  • Total XP
  • Player tier or rank title
  • Game statistic update date

Authentication credentials and passwords are not publicly displayed.

7. Guest Mode

Orbit Switch can be played without creating a cloud-connected account. Guest Mode allows access to core gameplay without Google authentication.

Guest gameplay data may be stored locally on your device and may be lost if the App is uninstalled, app storage is cleared, or local game data is reset.

8. Third-Party Services

Google Identity Services

Google authentication services are used only when a player voluntarily chooses to sign in with Google.

Supabase

Orbit Switch uses Supabase services for backend functionality, including:

  • User authentication
  • Account sessions
  • Cloud-connected player profiles
  • Gameplay synchronization
  • Global Leaderboard functionality
  • Database storage

9. Advertising and Tracking

The current version of Orbit Switch does not intentionally include:

  • Third-party advertising networks
  • Personalized advertising
  • Behavioral advertising
  • Advertising SDKs
  • Firebase Analytics
  • Mixpanel
  • AppsFlyer
  • Adjust
  • Sentry
  • Cross-app behavioral tracking

If future versions introduce new data collection, analytics, advertising, or tracking technologies, this Privacy Policy may be updated accordingly.

10. Data Storage and Security

Gameplay information and necessary application settings may be stored locally in the App's private storage.

Cloud-connected account and supported gameplay information may be stored using Supabase backend infrastructure.

We use reasonable technical measures including:

  • HTTPS/TLS encrypted communication
  • Private application storage
  • Authentication-based access controls
  • Session management
  • Authentication token removal during sign-out
  • Backend security controls
While we take reasonable measures to protect information, no internet transmission or electronic storage method can guarantee absolute security.

11. Data Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide Orbit Switch functionality and maintain an active account, unless longer retention is necessary for legitimate purposes such as security, fraud prevention, system integrity, disaster recovery, or legal obligations.

12. Sign Out

Signing out is different from deleting an account.

When you sign out:

  • Your local authentication session is cleared
  • Locally stored authentication tokens are removed
  • Local application data may be reset according to App functionality
  • Your cloud account is not automatically deleted

To permanently remove your account and associated data, use the Account Deletion feature.

13. Account and Data Deletion

You can permanently delete your Orbit Switch account and associated application data.

In-App Deletion

Authenticated users can delete their account directly through:

Settings → Delete Account & Data

A confirmation step is displayed before permanent deletion is initiated.

You can also request account deletion through our external Account Deletion page.

View Account Deletion Information

14. Children's Privacy

Orbit Switch is designed as a general-audience game. We do not knowingly request unnecessary sensitive personal information from children.

Users should avoid including personally identifying information in public Callsigns or Usernames.

15. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time due to changes in App functionality, data practices, service providers, or applicable legal requirements.

When changes are made, the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page will be revised.
