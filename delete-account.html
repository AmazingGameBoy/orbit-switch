Delete Your Account in the App

The fastest and recommended method is to delete your account directly inside Orbit Switch.

1

What Happens When You Delete Your Account?

When account deletion is successfully completed, applicable Orbit Switch account information and associated game data are permanently removed.

  • Player profile and Callsign
  • Cloud-connected gameplay statistics
  • High scores and leaderboard information
  • Player XP and progression
  • Player level and legend stars
  • Cloud-synchronized game progress
  • Associated local application data
  • Local authentication session information

Important Information

Account deletion is permanent. Deleted Orbit Switch account data and gameplay progress may not be recoverable.

Deleting your Orbit Switch account does not delete or modify your Google Account.

After successful deletion, signing in again may create a new Orbit Switch account. Previously deleted gameplay data may not be restored.

Account Verification

To protect users from unauthorized deletion requests, we may need to verify that a deletion request is made by the legitimate account owner before processing an external deletion request.

Please provide only the information reasonably necessary to identify your Orbit Switch account. Do not send passwords or sensitive financial information by email.
