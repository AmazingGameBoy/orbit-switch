Delete Your Account in the App

The fastest and recommended way to delete your account is directly inside Orbit Switch while you are signed in to your account.

1
Open Settings Open Orbit Switch and navigate to the Settings menu.
2
Select Delete Account & Data Tap the "Delete Account & Data" option.
3
Confirm Permanent Deletion Review the warning and confirm the permanent deletion request.
Important: Account deletion is permanent and cannot normally be undone.

Can't Access the App?

What Data Is Deleted?

When an Orbit Switch account deletion is successfully completed, the applicable cloud account and associated application data linked to that account are deleted from the active application database, subject to any limited retention described in our Privacy Policy.

  • Player profile and Callsign
  • Cloud-connected gameplay statistics
  • Cloud-stored scores and leaderboard data associated with the account
  • Player XP and progression data
  • Player level and legend stars
  • Supported cloud-synchronized game progress
  • Authentication account associated with Orbit Switch
Local data stored on your device may need to be removed by clearing the App's storage or uninstalling the App, depending on the version and functionality of Orbit Switch.

Important Information

Account deletion is permanent. Deleted account data and gameplay progress may not be recoverable.

Deleting your Orbit Switch account does not delete, modify, or affect your separate Google Account.

If you later sign in again, a new Orbit Switch account may be created. Previously deleted account data may not be restored.

Account Verification for Email Requests

To help protect accounts from unauthorized deletion requests, we may need to verify that an external deletion request is made by the legitimate account owner before processing it.

Please provide only the information reasonably necessary to identify your Orbit Switch account. Never send your Google password, authentication password, payment information, or other unnecessary sensitive information by email.

Deletion Request Processing

In-app deletion requests are processed according to the functionality available in the App. For deletion requests submitted by email, we will review the request and may contact you if additional information is reasonably necessary to verify account ownership.

Some limited information may be retained where reasonably necessary for security, fraud prevention, backup recovery, or compliance with applicable legal obligations, as described in our Privacy Policy.
